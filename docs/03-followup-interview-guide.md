# Follow-up Interview Guide — Pain Story Participants

Purpose: these are the survey respondents who gave real, specific pain
stories or notable feature requests instead of generic answers. The goal of
these calls isn't more data points, it's depth — find out exactly what
happened, what would have prevented it, and whether what you're planning to
build (mortality/feed/egg logging, vaccination reminders, monthly summary)
actually matches what they'd have used in the moment. Treat these as
conversations, not a script — follow whatever they say further before
moving to the next question.

Trimmed to only the people directly tied to validating one of the 5 locked
v1 features or the pricing model — 6 conversations, not more than needed.

Conducted over WhatsApp text, not calls. Send one question at a time and
wait for a reply before sending the next — dropping 4-5 questions in one
message tends to get a short, half-answered response back, and you lose
the chance to follow up on whatever they actually say.
