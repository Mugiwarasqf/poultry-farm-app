# Poultry Farm Record-Keeping — Validation Questionnaire

Purpose: this survey exists to answer one question before any code gets
written — do Nigerian poultry farmers feel enough real pain around
record-keeping, disease reporting, and access to credit that they'd actually
adopt, and pay for, a digital tool that fixes it? It's built to test that
across the full range of the market, from backyard flocks up through
commercial operations near or above the mandatory HPAI-insurance threshold,
so the responses can show whether the pain — and the price farmers would
tolerate — actually differs by farm size, rather than assuming one answer
fits everyone. Aim for 5-10 minutes to complete; the open-ended questions
(11, 13, 15) are where the real signal lives, so don't let those get skipped.

How to use: copy each question into Google Forms one at a time (Forms has no
universal "import a doc" feature without a third-party add-on — this is
written so you can build it manually in ~15 minutes). A CSV version is also
provided alongside this file if your Forms add-on supports CSV import.

Form intro — paste this into the Google Form's description field, it's what
farmers will actually read before answering:

> Hi, I'm exploring a record-keeping tool built for poultry farmers in
> Nigeria, for mortality, feed tracking, laying cycle, records for egg
> crates, vaccinations, and the paperwork behind insurance and loans. Takes
> 5-10 minutes; there's no right or wrong answer, just your honest
> experience, whether you run a backyard flock or a large farm. Your contact
> info is only so I can follow up about an early version, it won't be
> shared. Thanks for your time.
